# Ultimate Content & UX Plan for Lubelski Klub Go

This plan synthesizes feedback from multiple expert personas, focusing purely on **Content, User Experience (UX), and Club Vibe**. The goal is to transform the website from a static digital noticeboard into an active, warm, and welcoming community portal that makes the first-time visitor feel instantly at home.

## 🌟 Part 1: Core Strategy & Tone
The site must shift its tone from "Informational Archive" to **"Friendly Invitation."**
*   **Vibe:** Enthusiastic, patient, non-judgmental, and inclusive. The primary message is: *"You don't need to know how to play—just come."*
*   **Primary Goal:** Increase first-time attendance by removing all barriers (fear of rules, confusion about logistics).
*   **Core Asset:** Real photos of people playing Go at the club are more valuable than any text.

## 🧭 Part 2: Proposed Sitemap & Information Architecture
The navigation must be clean and shallow (no more than three clicks deep).

1.  **Home (The Hook):** The immediate "What, When, Where, Why."
2.  **Newcomers (The Guide):** For the first-time visitor/parent.
3.  **Community & Events (The Proof):** Showcasing life and activity.
4.  **About Us (The Story):** History, mentors, and mission statement.
5.  **Contact:** Location and contact form.

## 📅 Part 3: Page-by-Page Content Overhaul

### 🏠 Home Page (Above the Fold) - The Immediate Answer
*   **Hero Section:** Large, high-quality photo of people playing together.
    *   **Headline:** "Lubelski Klub Go – Grać, Uczyć się, Spotykać." (Play, Learn, Connect.)
    *   **Subtext/CTA:** Prominent box answering: **Next Meeting:** Wednesday | 17:00–20:00. Join us! (Button links to Calendar).
    *   **Quick Sell:** Three icons with text emphasizing benefits: FREE | ALL AGES WELCOME | EQUIPMENT PROVIDED.
*   **Immediate Scroll Content:** A brief, punchy "What is Go?" explanation and a link directly to the **Newcomers Guide**.

### 🎓 Newcomers (The Conversion Funnel) - The Anti-Intimidation Page
*   **"First Visit: What to Expect" Section:** This should be the main focus.
    *   Step-by-step guide (e.g., Arrive → Find Room 14 → Meet Mentor → Learn on a small board).
    *   Address all common questions in an FAQ format: *Do I need to bring anything? Is it really free? What if my child is too young/too old?*
*   **Simple Rules Overview:** A highly visual, minimal guide. Do NOT copy the entire rules essay; instead, explain concepts (e.g., "Atari" with a diagram) and link out to deep technical resources only after the user shows interest.

### 👥 Community & Events - The Proof of Life
*   **Events Calendar:** Must be robust and easily updated (Google Calendar embed is ideal). Categorize events: *Regular Night, Workshop, Tournament.*
    *   **Event Listing:** Use a "Upcoming" block that automatically pulls the next 3-5 events.
    *   **Archive:** Keep past results visible but clearly labeled as history (e.g., "Tournament Results - Fall 2023").
*   **Gallery/News Feed:** A rotating, curated feed of recent photos and short event reports ("Last Wednesday's Tournament Recap," with winners featured).

### 🧑‍🏫 About Us & Mentors - The Human Touch
*   **Mission Statement:** Short (3 sentences) on the club's purpose.
*   **People Focus:** Profile photos and short, warm bios for key organizers/mentors. This builds trust and connection.
*   **History:** Condense any long history into a visually engaging timeline graphic with 3-4 major milestones.

## ✨ Part 4: High-Impact Feature Recommendations (Phase 2)
These ideas will make the club's website stand out as a modern leader in its field.

1.  **Interactive Go Board Demo:** Embed a simple, interactive board that allows users to play basic puzzles or learn core concepts right on the page without needing an account. This is a massive value-add for beginners.
2.  **The "Go in Lublin" Hub:** Position the club as the definitive local resource. Include links/information related to other Polish Go associations, local cultural centers (MDK), and regional events.
3.  **Member Spotlight / Ladder:** A lighthearted section showcasing current members (with their rank) and giving recognition to active participants.

---
*This plan prioritizes a seamless user journey over displaying every historical detail or technical rule.*
