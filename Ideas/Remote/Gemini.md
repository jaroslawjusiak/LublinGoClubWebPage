Rebuilding a club webpage is a great opportunity to shift the focus from a "digital flyer" to an active community hub. Looking at the current layout, the main goal should be modernizing the flow, making it incredibly easy for newcomers to take the first step, and giving current members a reason to keep visiting the site.

Here is a breakdown of what might be holding the current site back, followed by new features to make it a more engaging platform.

### What to Refine or Remove

* **The Full-Width Poster (`Plakat.jpg`):** A static poster works great on a physical bulletin board, but on a landing page, it pushes vital information out of view. You could replace this with a modern "Hero Section"—a clean, welcoming headline, a high-quality photo of a recent meeting, and a clear call-to-action button (e.g., "Join Our Next Meeting").
* **Images inside the Navigation Bar:** The current navigation includes a Go board image nested inside the menu structure. Navigation should be strictly functional (Home, About, Meetings, Learning) to keep it clean, especially for mobile users.
* **All-Caps Announcements:** The "SPOTKANIA SĄ DARMOWE !!!" text is a crucial selling point, but the styling feels a bit dated. This can be converted into a "Why Join Us?" section with sleek icons highlighting key perks: Free Entry, All Ages Welcome, and Equipment Provided.
* **Static "Rules of the Game" Page:** Reading raw rules can be intimidating for someone who just stumbled onto the site. This could be trimmed down to a brief overview, linking out to interactive tutorials rather than hosting walls of text.

### New Features & Content Ideas

* **A Structured "Beginner Academy" Section:** Newcomers often fear they will be left to fend for themselves. You could dedicate a section to a formal learning path. Showcasing a structured curriculum—such as a step-by-step syllabus designed to take players from 30 kyu to 10 kyu over a series of lectures—demonstrates that they will receive organized, high-quality instruction at the youth centre.
* **OGS (Online Go Server) Integration:** Connect the physical club with your digital activity. By tapping into the OGS API, you could display a live club leaderboard, showcase recent games played by members, or highlight community stats directly on the homepage.
* **Interactive Game Viewer:** Embed a web-based SGF viewer. You could feature a weekly Go problem (Tsumego), showcase an interesting game played at the last Wednesday meeting, or upload classic historical matches accompanied by AI analysis insights.
* **"Your First Visit" FAQ:** Break down the barrier to entry for parents and complete beginners. Answer practical questions like: *Do I need to know how to play? Do I need to bring my own board? What is the best age for kids to start?*
* **Dynamic "Club Life" Feed:** Instead of a static gallery page, feature a dynamic section highlighting the Wednesday meetings at MDK nr 2. Showing parents, teenagers, and adults learning and playing together instantly sells the family-friendly atmosphere of the club.
