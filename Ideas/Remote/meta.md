Your current page is actually a perfect starting point for this exercise. It's so minimal that it tells us exactly what to keep.

For everyone reading this who hasn't seen it, the whole site right now is:

> **Lubelski Klub GO**
> **Środy 17.00–20.00**
> MDK nr 2, ul. Bernardyńska 14a, Sala 14
> Zapraszamy dzieci, młodzież i dorosłych...
> Link to zasady gry

That's it. So there is almost nothing to *remove*, but a lot to rewrite and expand.

Here is how I would think about it:

### 1. What I would remove / rewrite from the current content

**Nothing to delete completely, but everything needs a rewrite:**

*   **The broken sentence:** `Zapraszamy... Chętnie zapoznamy z grą rodziców oraz ich pociechy.` is split into two lines mid-sentence. It reads like a bug.
*   **The generic invitation:** `Uczymy się i bawimy razem, często całymi rodzinami.` - it's warm, but it says nothing concrete. What does a Wednesday look like? Who actually comes?
*   **The lonely link to rules:** `Zachęcamy do zapoznania się z zasadami gry.` is your only CTA and it sends people *away* to learn rules. For a beginner, rules are scary. The first CTA should be "come and we will show you".
*   **The 1990s information architecture:** All info is in one flat block. There is no hierarchy for a first-time visitor vs. a parent vs. a returning player.

I would keep the core facts - Wednesday, MDK2, Sala 14, families welcome - but make them part of a real structure.

### 2. What I would add - Core Content (Must Have)

Think of 3 different visitors: **Curious Adult, Parent of an 8-year-old, and a player who already knows Go.**

**A. For the Curious Adult - Hook in 30 seconds**
*   **What is Go? In one paragraph + one image.** Not the history from 4000 years ago. One line: "Chess is a battle, Go is a conversation." 2-sentence explanation + a beautiful board photo.
*   **Why come to us?** 3 bullet benefits: no equipment needed, no sign-up, learn your first game in 15 minutes. Strategy, calm, community.
*   **First Visit Guide / Pierwsza wizyta:** This is the most important new page. "Do I need to register? No. How much? Free. What to bring? Nothing. Will I be the only beginner? No, every Wednesday we teach from zero."

**B. For the Parent**
*   **Dzieci i młodzież - dedicated section.** Parents won't leave kids at "Sala 14 (Galeria na górze)". They want to know: Who is the instructor? (Kamil Jerzyk - you have a great asset here), Is it safe/supervised?, What age is ok?, Do parents stay? You already have "całymi rodzinami" - make it a feature, not a footnote.
*   **Where and How to find us.** Address is not enough. MDK2 is a big building. Photo of the entrance, which staircase, how to get to Sala 14, bus/tram, parking, accessibility. An embedded map.

**C. For the Community**
*   **Who we are / Poznaj nas.** 2-3 photos from a real Wednesday meeting. Not stock Go photos. Your people, your boards, tea. Short intro from the opiekun.
*   **Kontakt, który działa.** Right now there is no contact. Phone? Email? Facebook / Discord / Instagram? If someone has a question at 16:30 on Wednesday, how do they ask?

### 3. New Features - What makes a club site alive (Nice to Have -> Wow)

These are content ideas that turn a poster into a club homepage:

**To build trust and atmosphere:**
*   **Galeria z życia klubu** - 12 real photos > 1000 words. People playing, kids learning, close-ups of hands and stones.
*   **Głosy członków** - 2-3 short quotes: "Przyszedłem na jedną środę..." - social proof works incredibly well for niche hobbies.
*   **Co się u nas dzieje?** - Not a full blog, just 3 last news: "Byliśmy na turnieju w...", "Wakacje - nie ma spotkań 15.08", "Mamy nowe 9x9 dla dzieci".

**To lower the barrier:**
*   **FAQ - 6 questions that everyone asks:**
    > Czy muszę umieć grać? / Ile to kosztuje? / Od jakiego wieku? / Czy muszę przyjść z dzieckiem? / Co jeśli przyjdę w połowie? / Czy mogę tylko popatrzeć?
*   **Zasady gry - your own version.** Don't link to Wikipedia. Make a 3-minute version with diagrams: Atari, territory, "you win by surrounding". Interactive board would be a wow-feature later.
*   **English section.** Lublin has thousands of foreign students. A short EN version: "We speak English, beginners welcome" can double your audience.

**To keep people coming back:**
*   **Kalendarz** - Google Calendar embed: regular Wednesdays + tournaments, workshops, no-meeting days.
*   **Co dalej? / Ścieżka rozwoju** - "You learned the rules on Wednesday. What's next?" Links to OGS, books in our small library, next steps in the club.
*   **Turnieje i warsztaty** - Archive of past events + announcement of next. Even if it's just "Jedziemy na Lubelskie Dni Go".
*   **Sprzęt klubowy** - "We have 10 boards, you don't need your own. If you want your own, we can advise."

`Home (Hero + When/Where + CTA) / Pierwsza wizyta / Dla dzieci i rodziców / Zasady w 3 minuty / Galeria i życie klubu / Kalendarz / Kontakt`

Clean, warm, photo-heavy, no walls of text.
