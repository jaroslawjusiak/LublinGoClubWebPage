- The rules essay as a flat wall of text. - Raw personal phone number for a public club page — consider a club email/Discord only, or make phone opt-in. Also fix inconsistent Discord invites (home vs kontakt use different links).
Keep and upgrade
- The poster and hand-drawn art — that's your brand, not generic Go clip-art. Give it a proper home.
- Meeting logistics — but restructure it as an answer to "how do I show up?": day, hours, address, room, how to find the room, cost, "do I need to bring anything," "can I just come."
- Social links — Discord is a big asset for a small club; surface it as a real community hub, not a tiny icon.
New content / features worth considering
 1. "What is Go?" in 60 seconds — the single most important missing page. One hero line, one board image, one 90-second video or GIF, then "learn more."
 2. "First visit / what to expect" — beginner's on-ramp: no experience needed, we teach, boards and stones provided, come alone, bring a friend, kids welcome. This converts far more people than rules.
 3. "For families & kids" — you explicitly welcome parents and children; that's a differentiator (MDK venue, room 14). Speak to it directly.
 4. Living events section — upcoming club nights, tournaments, festivals, "last Wednesday we had X players." Even a manually maintained calendar beats stale news. Consider a Google Calendar embed.
 5. Gallery that actually exists — club nights, tournaments, faces (with consent). Photos of real people at the table are your best recruitment tool.
 6. Beginner resources — links to OGS, a "play your first game online" note, recommended apps/YouTube, maybe a small glossary (oddechy, atari, ko, oczy, terytorium).
 7. "Where we play online" — Discord + OGS handles so people can stay connected between Wednesdays.
 8. FAQ — is it free (yes), do I need my own board (no), do I need to know rules (no), age range, is it only Wednesdays, do you speak English.
 9. English version / language toggle — Lublin has students, expats, and Ukrainian neighbors; Go is a universal language. A EN toggle is cheap and high-value.
10. People, not just contact info — short bios for Piotr/Jarek (why they play, rank, what they teach), so newcomers see humans.
11. Join/CTA block — "Come on Wednesday" with map link, Discord button, and a one-click Add to calendar (.ics).
12. Partners & context — MDK nr 2, Polski Związek Go, Szaleni Samuraje — establishes legitimacy.
Content principles I'd apply
- Every page should answer one visitor question; no page should be "under construction."
- Home page = who, what, when, where, why + one strong CTA in the first screen.
- Rules = staged learning, not a reference dump.
- Freshness beats completeness: 3 current posts > 10 old ones.
