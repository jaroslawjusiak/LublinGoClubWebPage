# Club brand assets

## Official club logo

`club-logo.svg` is the official club logo supplied by the owner as
`docs/design/reference/Logo.png` and traced into
`docs/design/reference/Logo.svg` with VTracer. This production copy preserves
the SVG's portrait proportions, transparent background and black/white stones.
The shared `BrandMark` component uses it as the primary identity in the header
and footer. A small paper background keeps the original colors visible in the
dark footer.

The logo is decorative beside the visible club name; the containing link has
the accessible club name.

## Retained goban mark

`goban-mark.svg` is an original vector drawn for the Papier i goban direction on
2026-09-30. It is not extracted from the generated concept. The fragment has five
regular horizontal and vertical lines spaced 12 units apart. The black and white
stones are centered on intersections (20,20) and (44,44), with diameter 15 units,
matching the modest overlap of adjacent Go stones.

No third-party artwork or fonts are embedded. This original candidate mark is
retained unchanged for possible future use; it is no longer the primary website
logo. When used decoratively beside the club name, hide it from assistive
technology and give the containing link its accessible club name.
