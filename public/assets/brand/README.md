# Goban mark

`goban-mark.svg` is an original vector drawn for the Papier i goban direction on
2026-09-30. It is not extracted from the generated concept. The fragment has five
regular horizontal and vertical lines spaced 12 units apart. The black and white
stones are centered on intersections (20,20) and (44,44), with diameter 15 units,
matching the modest overlap of adjacent Go stones.

No third-party artwork or fonts are embedded. This candidate mark is prepared for
the shared brand component in V3. When used decoratively beside the club name,
hide it from assistive technology and give the containing link its accessible
club name. No page currently references the asset.
